Scoring matrix

Pick a score in the ranking key, then move across each row and click the squares to score how the row agent affects the column agent. Clicking a square that already has the chosen score clears it. With a mouse, drag to score several squares at once. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys, type 1 2 3 for a positive score or - then a number for a negative one, and Delete to clear. A small ring marks an interaction from your Interaction Matrix that has no score yet.
